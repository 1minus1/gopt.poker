Rules Before 7/21/2015

  1. Chip hits floor (including dealer chip, not including the banker when he is setting up the game or chipping up) = 1 shot
  2. Win 4 hands in a row = 1 shot
  3. Sloppy cards (on floor) = 1 shot
  4. Spill beer/spill drink = 1 shot
  5. Sloppy pour = 1 shot
  6. Splashes off bottles/bombs are not a shot
  7. Watching TV (when not specifically allowed by the host) = 1 shot
  8. Sleeping at poker = 2 shots
  9. 1/2 wins (split pots) count as a win
  10. What happens/is said at poker stays at poker
  11. Missing poker/canceling on game day (less than 24 hours notice) = 1 shot. Not notifying the host = +1 shot
  12. If your wife/significant other calls/texts and you answer it = 1 shot. If you call her = 1 shot
  13. Missed deal = 1 shot
  14. Penalty shots must be drank before the first deal (those earned the previous month) or the next deal (those earned on a given night)
  15. Sloppy food (food is dropped on floor) = 1 shot
  16. Leaving poker before everyone is finished or it is before 11:00 PM = 2 shots
  17. No food instigation
  18. Bringing your underage child (< 18 years old) to poker = 2 deferred shots
  19. Must reveal your entire hand when called = 1 shot
  20. No blockading any door with a potentially falling chip to force a shot
  21. Taking cards to the loo = 1 shot
  22. Shots deferred to the next month/gathering = 2x the number of shots deferred. A maximum of 4 shots are allowed to be deferred (doubling to 8 at the next gathering) before the option of deferring is no longer allowed.
  23. Shots don’t start accruing on a given night until the first deal
  24. If you are the host and you run out of alcohol = 2 shots. If you supply a 12-pack the next poker night you only owe 1 shot.
  25. If you take a card out of turn = 1 shot.
  26. More than 1 hour late (even if you say you will be late) = 1 shot
  27. Any grey area regarding a chip that hits the floor goes to the last person that touched the chip = 1 shot
  28. Premature drop in guts = add a nickel to the pot
  29. Carrying a purse at poker = 1 shot
  30. Winner takes cards home and is responsible for bringing them the next poker night. Failure to do so = 1 shot
  31. Failure to wear the monthly championship bracelet in public while still the reigning champion = 1 shot
  32. Three consecutive bracelet wins = 1 shot
  33. If you slow roll less than a dollar pot = 1 shot
  34. Four GOPT members are necessary for any event to count towards the official GOPT record books.
  35. No consulting the matrix prior to the completion of a tournament.
  36. Failing to pay your entry fee on a given night prior to cards being dealt is a shot.
  37. 20*(2n-P1-P2)/(n–1) is a person’s payout for a standard non-major night, where P1 and P2 are the individual places in each tourney, and n is the number of players. This is equal to the number of points awarded toward season ranking. Other scenarios can be handled using the GOPT Payout Schedule Technical Memo (http://gopt.poker/files/GOPTpayouttechnote.pdf).
  38. Points awarded toward season rankings from a major is equal to 80*(n-P)/(n–1), where n is the number of players and P is the place in the event. The place is determined in a single-tournament event by order of finishing. In a multi-tournament event it is determined by first, the sum of earnings and second, the average of ranks in each tournament.
  39. Should there be a tie based on the use of Rule 37 or Rule 38, the better-ranked person is the one with the larger payout and/or better place in the last tournament of the event in question.
